OFFICE LEASE AGREEMENT

This Office Lease Agreement ("Lease") is made between Rosslyn Tower Owner LLC
("Landlord") and Meridian Analytics, LLC ("Tenant").

1. PREMISES. Landlord leases to Tenant approximately 8,500 rentable square feet
   on the 6th floor of the building located at 1700 North Moore Street.

2. TERM. The Lease Commencement Date is May 1, 2023. The Lease shall expire on
   April 30, 2028, unless sooner terminated in accordance with this Lease.

3. BASE RENT. Tenant shall pay annual base rent of $340,000, payable in equal
   monthly installments of $28,333.33.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Lease.
